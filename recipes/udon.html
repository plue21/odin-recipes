Udon Noodle Soup Recipe

Description

A simple Japanese noodle dish featuring thick, chewy udon noodles served in a warm, savory dashi broth. Light, comforting, and full of delicate umami flavor. It is also called "Kake Udon" and is the simplest form of udon that lets you appreciate the texture of the noodles and the flavor of the broth.

Ingredients

  • Frozen Udon noodles
  • Homemade soup broth (but if need a quick broth):
    • Dashi (Japanese cooking stock used to add a savory umami flavor)
    • Mirin (a sweet Japanese rice wine)
    • Soy sauce
    • Kosher salt (a coarse cooking salt with large, flaky crystals)
    • Sugar
  • Green onions for the garnish

Steps

  1. Prepare the udon noodles according to the package instructions. Drain them and set them aside.
  2. For the broth:
    • Add 2 and 1/3 cups dashi
    • 1 and 1/2 tablespoons soy sauce
    • 1 tablespoon mirin
    • 1 tablespoon mirin
    • 1/8 teaspoon kosher salt in a different pot
    • bring the broth to a gentle simmer.
  3. Taste the broth and adjust the seasoning if needed.
  4. Place the cooked udon noodles into serving bowls.
  5. Pour the hot broth over the noodles.
  6. Top with sliced green onions and any other toppings you prefer.
  7. Serve immediately while the noodles and broth are hot.
