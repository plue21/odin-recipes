Cheeseburger Recipe

Description

A juicy grilled beef patty topped with melted cheese, fresh lettuce, ripe tomatoes, and tangy pickles, all nestled in a soft, toasted bun.

Ingredients

  • 160g ground beef
  • 2 slices of cheddar cheese (you can add more slices according to your preference)
  • 2 hamburger buns
  • Lettuce, tomato, onion for the toppings (however, add as much as you want)
  • Salt, sugar and pepper for the seasoning
  • Mayonnaise and chili sauce (e.g. Sriracha)

Steps

  1. The toppings should be sliced thin to put on the patty.
  2. Preheat a grill or skillet over medium-high heat.
  3. Form the ground beef into a patty and season with salt and pepper.
  4. Cook the patties for 3-4 minutes per side, or until desired doneness.
  5. Toast the hamburger buns on the grill or skillet.
  6. Simultaniously put thin-sliced onions in a pan with butter.
    • Add a pinch of salt and add 1/3 teaspoon sugar. Cook the onions down until tender.
    • Reduce the heat to low-medium, stir them to you prefered tenderness and be careful not to burn them.
    • Place the two slices of cheese on the patty, let it melt and cover the patty.
  7. For the sauce, mix mayo and chili sauce 2:1 until the color turns orange.
  8. Assemble the burgers with lettuce, tomato, caramelized onions, and the cooked patty.
  9. Serve immediately and enjoy!
